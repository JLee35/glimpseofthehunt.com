01 — Getting Started

Bring it in or freeze it immediately.

Your trophy needs to be brought to the shop or frozen immediately. If frozen, keep skin to skin and hair to hair, and try to hide the lips, eyes, and ears inside the cape to protect these sensitive areas.

My goal is to have your trophy back in nine months so you can display your dream. Thanks for your business and good luck hunting!
